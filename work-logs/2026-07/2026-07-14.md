- Split SNS topics into trigger topic (raw CloudWatch state changes, Lambda only) and delivery topic (formatted notifications, email) — eliminates duplicate emails per alarm.
- Renamed dashboard row from `Alerts & Incidents` to `Alarms & Notifications` for correct CloudWatch terminology.
- Updated `ALARM_PREFIX` to `IA-IT-SRE` pattern so state-publisher and re-notifier capture alarms across all 6 platforms.
