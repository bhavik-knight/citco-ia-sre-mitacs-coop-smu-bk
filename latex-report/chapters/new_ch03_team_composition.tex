\chapter{Team Composition \& Responsibilities}
\label{chap:team}

My placement was within the \textbf{Intelligent Automation \& Site Reliability Engineering (IA-SRE)} team at Citco Technology Management (CTM), Halifax. This chapter describes the team I worked with and the responsibilities I held during the co-op placement.


\FloatBarrier

\section{Team Structure}
\label{sec:team_structure}


The IA-SRE team is co-located across Halifax (Canada) and Hyderabad (India), with stakeholders spanning Dublin and London. My direct supervisor was Mr.\ Kishor Deotale (Manager, CTM). The co-op period team comprised the following members:

\begin{itemize}

    \item \textbf{Mr.\ Kishor Deotale} --- Manager, CTM and Industry Supervisor.
          Mr.\ Deotale is responsible for all work streams, sets engineering priorities,
          and served as the final acceptance authority for all deliverables.

    \item \textbf{Mr.\ Ho Hoi Leung (``Mr.\ Leung'')} --- IT Senior Developer, RPA Platform Lead.
          Mr.\ Leung is the primary knowledge holder for Blue Prism and UiPath
          platform operations, responsible for escalation triage in the
          Follow~the~Sun support model and the designated Halifax
          escalation point for production incidents.

    \item \textbf{Ms.\ Kristia Marie Labos (``Ms.\ Labos'')} --- IT Developer, SRE Engineer.
          Ms.\ Labos leads the SRE practice for the team, including the four
          golden signals framework. She guided the observability platform
          design and validated alarm threshold selection throughout the
          dashboard build.

    \item \textbf{Mr.\ Sridhar Vadla and Ms.\ Soundarya Venkataraman} --- Co-interns
          (SMU MCDA, Mitacs BSI IT49539). Both joined the Halifax team
          concurrently, each working on complementary workstreams of the
          IA monitoring and automation platform, enabling parallel progress
          across the observability roadmap.
\end{itemize}


\FloatBarrier

\section{My Role and Responsibilities}
\label{sec:my_role}


As a participant in the Mitacs Business Strategy Internship (BSI) program, I was embedded
within the IA-SRE team at CTM Halifax. I collaborated closely with Mr.\ Deotale, Mr.\ Leung,
Ms.\ Labos, co-interns Mr.\ Vadla and Ms.\ Venkataraman, and stakeholders across Halifax, Hyderabad, and
Dublin. Weekly standups, sprint reviews, and direct Confluence and JIRA-based
collaboration formed the day-to-day working rhythm.

My responsibilities spanned the following areas:

\begin{itemize}
    \item \textbf{IA Infrastructure Monitoring:} Developing per-platform CloudWatch
          dashboards for Lambda, ECS, EC2, SQS, EKS, and related AWS services across
          all six platforms, establishing the architectural patterns for dynamic resource
          discovery.

    \item \textbf{SRE Observability Platform Development:} Designing and building the
          centralized Amazon Managed Grafana (AMG) dashboard covering all AWS services
          across the six IA-IT-SRE Infrastructure platforms, including CloudFormation
          alarm provisioning and Infrastructure-as-Code delivery.

    \item \textbf{Inventory API Development:} Building the
          \texttt{ia-it-sre-infrastructure-inventory} Flask REST API for programmatic
          discovery of all deployed AWS resources across the IA-IT-SRE Infrastructure,
          with a discoverer registry pattern that provides a live, per-platform inventory
          of all AWS resources by service category.

    \item \textbf{Cloud Infrastructure Engineering:} Authoring parameterized
          CloudFormation and AWS SAM templates, managing IAM roles for cross-account
          Grafana access, enabling CloudWatch Container Insights on ECS clusters, and
          navigating AWS service constraints encountered during delivery.

    \item \textbf{Scrum and Meeting Facilitation:} Leading daily standup calls on a
          rotation basis. Attending dashboard design reviews, knowledge-transfer
          sessions (RPA platforms, CitcoWorks, Event Store, \AE xeo), security
          and compliance reviews, and stakeholder syncs with the wider CTM team.

    \item \textbf{JIRA Story Creation and Management:} Translating verbal
          requirements into structured JIRA stories and tasks with clear
          acceptance criteria and subtask decomposition. Raising defect tickets
          for issues identified through root cause analysis of production incidents.

    \item \textbf{RPA Production Support:} Providing operational support for
          Blue Prism and UiPath automation processes during dedicated support
          rotation weeks (11~AM--7~PM~EST shift, rotating weekly among four team members).
          Responsibilities included monitoring bot queues, triaging service desk
          tickets, onboarding new clients for Corporate Actions workflows,
          retriggering failed processes, and escalating where required.

    \item \textbf{Incident Response and Hotfix Engineering:} Investigating and
          resolving production incidents including the CAIS Deadline credential
          failure, MESO month-end stuck tasks, and VPL
          pricing failures through root cause analysis and
          targeted code or configuration fixes.

    \item \textbf{AI-Augmented Engineering:} Leveraging the Kiro IDE, Amazon~Q, and
          Claude Sonnet for spec-driven development, automated code scaffolding,
          CloudFormation template generation, and test suite creation.

    \item \textbf{Technical Documentation:} Maintaining Confluence knowledge-base
          pages for Lambda function inventories, runbooks, incident post-mortems, and
          architecture decisions.
\end{itemize}

These responsibilities required a blend of cloud engineering expertise, operational
discipline, and cross-functional communication skills to deliver production-grade
monitoring infrastructure while maintaining the reliability of live financial
automation processes.
