\section{The Citco Group of Companies}

The Citco Group of Companies (Citco) is the world's largest independent hedge fund administrator, managing over \$1.8 trillion in assets under administration. Founded in 1948 as the \textit{Curaçao International Trust Company} in Curaçao, Netherlands Antilles, the firm now operates from its headquarters in Tortola, British Virgin Islands. Over more than seven decades, Citco has grown from a civil law notary practice into a global financial services conglomerate with 50+ offices worldwide and approximately 16,000 employees across North America, Europe, Asia-Pacific, and the Caribbean.

Citco remains majority independently owned --- a distinction that underpins its client-centric operating philosophy. CEO Christopher Smeets leads a management team that has overseen sustained organic growth, from \$250 billion in administered assets in 2005 to today's \$1.8 trillion benchmark. This growth reflects Citco's pioneering role in shaping the fund administration sector and its continuous investment in proprietary technology platforms.

\subsection{Core Service Divisions}

Citco's global operations are organized across several specialized divisions:

\begin{itemize}
    \item \textbf{Citco Fund Services (CFS) \& Investor Relations} --- The flagship division and global leader in services for the alternative investment industry. CFS provides full-service fund administration, fund accounting, net asset value (NAV) calculations, investor relations, and reporting for over 2,000 funds. CFS has maintained SOC~1 Type~2 certification for 22 consecutive years and achieved its inaugural SOC~2 Type~2 certification in 2025, underscoring its commitment to operational controls and data security.

    \item \textbf{Citco Governance Services} --- Provides corporate and global subsidiary governance, secretarial services, financial accounting and reporting, tax and audit liaison, entity incorporation, and market entry services across multiple jurisdictions.

    \item \textbf{Citco Banking} --- Offers clients a comprehensive suite of banking services including investor accounts, operational and deposit bank accounts, foreign exchange, and credit services.

    \item \textbf{Citco Technology Management (CTM)} --- The internal technology arm responsible for developing and maintaining Citco's proprietary technology ecosystem, cloud infrastructure, data platforms, and enterprise applications that power fund administration and middle-office operations globally.

    \item \textbf{Middle Office Solutions} --- Encompassing Treasury and Collateral Management, OTC settlements, Trade Matching, and Portfolio Finance. Citco's cloud-based \AE xeo\textsuperscript{\textregistered} Treasury platform provides centralized wire approval and fund movement visibility.
\end{itemize}

\subsection{Industry Recognition}

Citco consistently receives industry accolades, including the Hedgeweek European Award for Fund Administrator of the Year (Middle Office Services, 2026), Best Administrator over \$30 billion (APAC Hedge Fund Services Awards, 2026), and Fund Administrator of the Year for GPs over \$30 billion (Private Equity Wire US Awards, 2025). These awards reflect both depth of service and breadth of technological innovation.

\section{Citco Technology Management --- Halifax, Canada}

My internship placement is within \textbf{Citco Technology Management (CTM)}, the technology division of the Citco Group. The Halifax, Nova Scotia office at 5151 George Street serves as a significant operational center for Citco Canada Inc., occupying multiple floors and functioning as a hub for technology development, fund administration services, and operational support for North American and global clients.

CTM designs, develops, deploys, and maintains Citco's internal technology platforms. This includes enterprise application development, cloud infrastructure management, DevOps and Site Reliability Engineering (SRE), data engineering pipelines, and intelligent automation initiatives. The Halifax CTM team collaborates with Citco colleagues across global offices to deliver technology solutions that underpin the firm's fund administration, investor relations, and governance services.
